Features

Everything you need at the edge

A single platform to route, cache, protect, and encrypt your traffic across any infrastructure.

Load Balancing

Distribute traffic intelligently across backends with round-robin, least-connections, and IP-hash algorithms. Health checks keep your upstreams reliable.

Edge Caching

Serve content from the network edge with configurable cache rules per route. Reduce origin load and deliver sub-millisecond response times globally.

WAF & Security

Block malicious traffic with built-in web application firewall rules, rate limiting, geo-blocking, and IP allow/deny lists at the edge.

Auto SSL

Automatic certificate provisioning and renewal via Let's Encrypt. Zero-downtime HTTPS everywhere with OCSP stapling and HTTP/2 out of the box.
